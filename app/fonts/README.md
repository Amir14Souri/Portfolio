# Local fonts

Geist and Geist Mono are distributed under the SIL Open Font License in
`OFL.txt`. These WOFF2 files retain variable weights 100–900 and are subset
to Latin, punctuation, arrows, and the symbols used by the site, including
the typing cursor. They are bundled by `next/font/local`; building and
viewing the site do not contact Google Fonts.

Geist 1.800 comes from the official `geist@1.7.2` package. Geist Mono 1.701 comes
from the [Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geistmono). FontTools was used to subset
the originals; these tools are not required to build the site.
